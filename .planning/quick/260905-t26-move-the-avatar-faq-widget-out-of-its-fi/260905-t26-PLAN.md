<objective>
Move the AI video avatar FAQ widget out of its fixed bottom-right floating popup and embed it in place inside the Home page's Manifesto section left column (the whitespace under the Crest/Laurel graphic, left of the "Welcome to LaDidiér…" paragraph), and gate its first playback behind first-scroll-into-view instead of page mount.

Purpose: the widget currently hijacks the viewport corner on every Home visit and starts a video the instant the page mounts. Embedding it in the Manifesto column makes it part of the editorial page rather than an overlay, and scroll-gating means the intro plays when the visitor arrives at that section — not while they are still reading the hero.

Output: a widget that is an ordinary in-flow block in the Manifesto column, with the `createPortal` escape hatch removed (it existed only to serve `position: fixed`), and an `IntersectionObserver` mount gate in front of the video engine.
</objective>

Today `useVideoAvatar()` runs unconditionally at the top of `AIAvatarFAQ`, and its `useReducer(avatarReducer, hasIntroPlayedThisSession(), initAvatarState)` lazy-initializes straight into the `intro` phase — so the intro clip starts the instant the component mounts. Gate that at the **mount level**, not inside the hook or the reducer.

Split `AIAvatarFAQ` into two components in this same file:

**Inner — `AvatarWidgetContent` (new, NOT exported).** Move the entire current body of `AIAvatarFAQ` into it: the `useVideoAvatar()` destructure, `<AvatarVideo … />`, the `{blocked && <button className="avatar-faq-tap-to-play" …>}` branch, and `<FAQOverlay … />` with all of its existing props. It returns those three as a fragment — it does **not** render the `.avatar-faq-widget` div, which now belongs to the wrapper. No prop or handler wiring changes; this is a relocation, not a rewrite.

**Outer — `AIAvatarFAQ` (same name, so pages.jsx's import and usage are untouched).** It:
- holds `const widgetRef = useRef(null)` and `const [hasBeenVisible, setHasBeenVisible] = useState(false)`;
- always renders `<div className="avatar-faq-widget" ref={widgetRef}>` — immediately, on mount, even while empty. The CSS from Task 1 already gives that div its size, so it reserves its slot in the Manifesto column from first paint and the video appearing later causes no layout shift;
- renders `{hasBeenVisible && <AvatarWidgetContent />}` as its only child;
- runs one `useEffect` with **empty deps** that creates a `new IntersectionObserver(…, { threshold: 0.3 })` on `widgetRef.current`, and on the first entry with `isIntersecting` true calls `setHasBeenVisible(true)` and then `observer.disconnect()`. Return a cleanup that also disconnects (idempotent — safe if the callback already did).
- Guard for the observer being unavailable: if `typeof IntersectionObserver === 'undefined'`, call `setHasBeenVisible(true)` and return — degrading to exactly today's play-on-mount behaviour is the correct fallback, silently never playing is not.

Use the native browser API only. No new dependency, no new file.

**Inline comments are required here** — this is the mount-gating replacement for the portal comment Task 1 removed, and it is the "why is this component shaped oddly" note for the next reader. Cover:
- Why the split exists at all: the video engine and the reducer must not initialize until first scroll-visibility. Because `useVideoAvatar`'s `useReducer` lazy-inits to the `intro` phase, merely *hiding* the content or gating inside the hook would still start it — only not mounting the child does.
- Why empty deps are correct: the effect binds once, nothing inside it reads changing state (so there is no stale-closure risk), and the observer disconnects itself after its single fire so the effect never needs to re-run.
- Why the observer disconnects after one fire and `hasBeenVisible` never resets: "starts once" is session-scoped and already owned by `hasIntroPlayedThisSession`/`markIntroPlayed`; the observer's only job is "has the visitor arrived here yet", and re-firing would let it fight the reducer for control of playback.
- The explicit non-goal: scrolling the section back out of view does **not** pause, and scrolling back in does **not** restart. Only the first transition into view matters — it is a like-for-like replacement for what "on mount" used to mean. Continuous visibility tracking is deliberately out of scope.
- Note that `IntersectionObserver` fires an initial callback on `observe()` with the current intersection state, so a visitor who lands already scrolled to that section (restored scroll position, deep link) gets the intro immediately rather than having to scroll away and back.

Do not touch `avatarReducer.js`, `avatarReducer.test.js`, `avatarFaqData.js`, the `AVATAR_MOBILENAV_EVENT` listener, `hasIntroPlayedThisSession`/`markIntroPlayed`, `AvatarVideo`, `FAQPill`, `FAQOverlay`, or any CSS. The export statement stays exactly `export { AIAvatarFAQ };` — `AvatarWidgetContent` is file-internal.
